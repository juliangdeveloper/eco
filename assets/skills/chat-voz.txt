Voice chat

When it applies: the person wants to talk to Eco by voice, dictate, have Eco read its replies aloud or change how listening works.

What exists: Settings › Voice has two independent switches. Listen (microphone, continuous listening and the wake word) only appears when the device has a usable microphone; when it is off or missing the header microphone is hidden. Speak (system voices or the optional Piper voice) only appears when the device has voice output. The header microphone turns on continuous listening. Voice detection and transcription run on the device (Silero VAD and Whisper in the browser; the transcriber downloads once and unloads when nobody speaks); no audio goes to any speech service. Eco only gets a message when the person says its name (Settings › Name, Eco by default; give each Eco in the same room its own name) and receives only what was said after it. Tapping the microphone works like saying the name; tapping it again before speaking turns listening off. After Eco finishes speaking there's a short follow-up window where the name isn't needed (set in Settings › Voice). If Listen was on and the microphone permission is already granted, listening starts again after a reload without a tap when the browser allows it; otherwise it starts on the next tap. On iPhone and iPad the permission often returns to prompt after a reload, so a tap may be needed again. When both speech and listening are waiting for a tap, Eco starts speaking in that tap and then opens the microphone. If Barge-in is on (Settings › Voice, default on), talking while Eco is speaking stops the voice and is taken as a reply without the wake name; raise the barge-in threshold if Eco hears itself. While a question with options is open, saying the option or its number ("the second", "2") answers it. Replies to voice turns are spoken with the system voices, sentence by sentence, skipping code, tables and links with a short note that the details are in the chat; saying the name while it speaks interrupts it. An optional local neural voice (Piper) downloads about 75 MB the first time. Listening turns itself off after a while without hearing the name, and it only works while Eco is open in the foreground.

Settings: Settings › Voice has the transcriber (Whisper tiny or base), speech language (Automatic uses the device language, or a chosen language such as Spanish or English), system voice, neural voice, phrase and turn silence, max phrase, transcriber unload time, auto-off time, follow-up window, detection threshold, speech rate and pitch and name match tolerance, with Reset to defaults.

The configured name wakes Eco. Magic phrases set in Settings › Magic also trigger while Listen is on, without the name; see the Magic skill. A shorter word such as “panel” does not wake a longer name such as “panelo”. Voice problems (transcriber not loading, failed transcription, microphone errors) stay on screen until voice recovers or the person dismisses them. voz_estado reads the current state without changing anything, with listen and speak reported separately: support, switch, phase, whether voice detection and the transcriber are loaded, the model, the name and wake words, the last transcriptions with times and the last error; use it before answering questions about why voice isn't working. If the microphone is blocked, tell the person to allow it in the browser. Don't claim the transcript is perfect: small local models can mishear names and places.

hablar says text aloud even when listening is off; it only exists when the device has voice output. Use it when the person or a shared-memory message asks for something to be said aloud. If it returns status disabled, Speak is off: tell the requester nothing was said. Status queued means the browser needs a first tap before it can play; it plays on that tap. escuchar turns the microphone on or off (action on or off) and is available when the device has a microphone; use it after hablar asks a question so you can hear the answer, optionally with follow_up in seconds to accept a reply without the wake name after Eco finishes speaking. If it returns status queued, listening starts on the next tap, after any queued speech has started.
