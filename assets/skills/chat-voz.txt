Voice chat

When it applies: the person wants to talk to Eco by voice, dictate, have Eco read its replies aloud or change how listening works.

What exists: the header microphone turns on continuous listening. Voice detection and transcription run on the device (Silero VAD and Whisper in the browser; the transcriber downloads once and unloads when nobody speaks); no audio goes to any speech service. Eco only gets a message when the person says its name (the "Name to call Eco" setting, Eco by default) and receives only what was said after it. Tapping the microphone works like saying the name; tapping it again before speaking turns listening off. After Eco finishes speaking there's a short follow-up window where the name isn't needed. While a question with options is open, saying the option or its number ("the second", "2") answers it. Replies to voice turns are spoken with the system voices, sentence by sentence, skipping code, tables and links with a short note that the details are in the chat; saying the name while it speaks interrupts it. An optional local neural voice (Piper) downloads about 75 MB the first time. Listening turns itself off after a while without hearing the name, and it only works while Eco is open in the foreground.

Settings: Settings › Voice has the name, transcriber (Whisper tiny or base), system voice, neural voice, phrase and turn silence, max phrase, transcriber unload time, auto-off time, follow-up window, detection threshold, speech rate and pitch and name match tolerance, with Reset to defaults.

If the microphone is blocked, tell the person to allow it in the browser. Don't claim the transcript is perfect: small local models can mishear names and places.
